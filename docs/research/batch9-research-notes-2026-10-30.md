## Massachusetts (DOR guide updated 9/15/2022; RMV pages; mass.gov blocks curl, read in the browser)
- 6.25%. Casual sale: use tax on the greater of the price or the clean trade-in (book) value, adjusted for mileage; condition does not lower it; salvage title taxed on price. https://www.mass.gov/guides/motor-vehicle-and-trailer-sales-and-use-tax
- No trade-in reduction on casual sales. Gift exempt (MVU-24) if donor paid MA tax, no payment, no debt assumed. Family (MVU-26): parent, spouse, child, sibling, if transferor paid MA tax. RMV: not grandparent, cousin, uncle, aunt. Gift: $25 non-refundable gift transfer fee. https://www.mass.gov/info-details/familygift-transfers
- Title and register within 10 days (DOR); RMV: title within 10 days. Inspection sticker within 7 days of registration. Insurance agent stamps the RTA form. Title $75. https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-a-registration-and-title-for-a-vehicle-purchased-from-an-individual
- Registration: passenger $60 for 2 years; motorcycle $20 a year; transfer registration $25. https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massachusetts-registry-of-motor-vehicles-fees (May 2025 list)
- Excise: $25 per $1,000 of value, value = % of list price (50% year before model year, 90%, 60%, 40%, 25%, then 10%), $5 minimum, billed by city or town. https://www.mass.gov/guides/motor-vehicle-excise
- New residents: register as soon as you become a resident, no grace period; MVU-29; exempt if purchased and registered in prior state more than 6 months while a resident. Out-of-state purchase within 6 months: credit for tax paid to a reciprocal state. https://www.mass.gov/how-to/transfer-your-registration-and-title-from-out-of-state
- Dead link: https://www.mass.gov/info-details/motor-vehicle-sales-and-use-tax

## Indiana (BMV, DOR)
- Fee chart revised 1/1/26 (raw text order): passenger $21.35, motorcycle $26.35, TIIF $15, EV supplemental $242, hybrid $81, title $15, late title penalty $30, transfer plate $9.50, late registration $15. https://www.in.gov/bmv/files/Fee_Chart.pdf
- Title within 45 days; seller keeps plate; private-sale sales tax paid at the branch; credit for tax paid to another state; new residents not charged sales tax. https://www.in.gov/bmv/titles/buying-and-selling
- ST-108E (R6 / 5-23): Ex. 7 gift/inheritance with no consideration (loan assumption is consideration); Ex. 8 new resident; Ex. 11 adding/deleting spouse, child, grandparent, parent, sibling (IC 6-2.5-5-15.5); trade-allowance line "like-kind exchanges only". https://forms.in.gov/Download.aspx?id=7261
- Excise tax: class by original retail price x age table, collected at registration; county and municipal excise/wheel taxes. https://www.in.gov/bmv/fees-taxes/vehicle-registration-fees-and-taxes/excise-tax-information
- New residents: 60 days for license and title (or Form 1014); VIN inspection at branch. https://www.in.gov/bmv/licenses-permits-ids/new-indiana-residents/
- Dead link: https://www.in.gov/bmv/registration-plates/vehicle-registration-fees/

## Missouri (DOR, RSMo, CSR)
- 4.225% + local where you live; local 1.0% (Bates, St. Clair, Vernon counties) to 7.263% (St. Ann, Pine Lawn) on the Oct to Dec 2026 MV chart (updated 8/25/2026). https://dor.mo.gov/forms/MV%20-%20Local%20Sales%20Tax.pdf
- Title $8.50 + $9 processing; 30 days, $25 penalty on day 31, +$25 per 30 days, max $200. Safety inspection unless under 11 years old and under 150,000 miles (exempt for first ten years after model year); emissions in St. Louis City, St. Louis, St. Charles, Jefferson counties; ID/OD inspection for out-of-state titles. https://dor.mo.gov/motor-vehicle/titling-registration/buying-vehicle.html
- Registration by taxable horsepower: 1-yr fees $18.25 (<12 HP or EV), $21.25, $24.25 (24-35), $33.25, $39.25, $45.25, $51.25 + $9 processing; motorcycle $8.75 + $9 (expires June, prorated). https://dor.mo.gov/motor-vehicle/titling-registration/fees.html
- Trade-in: DOR pages say tax on price less trade-in allowance; RSMo 144.025 does not limit to dealers; 12 CSR 10-103.350(3)(D) says trade-in applies only to dealer transactions. 180-day sold-vehicle credit (bill of sale or Form 5049). Gift: no tax, gift statement/affidavit.
- New residents: 30 days; no tax if titled in your name in another state more than 90 days; statement of non-assessment; safety + emissions inspections. https://dor.mo.gov/motor-vehicle/titling-registration/
- EV: alternative fuel decal RSMo 142.869, $75 base + 20% of base per year for five years from 2022 = $150 in 2026 (derived); plug-in hybrids (2018+) half = $75; electricity is an alternative fuel (142.800).
