# Motorcycle EV fees: agency replies adopted, batches 1 and 2

Cliff adopted these written agency replies on November 9 and 10, 2026. The replies are logged in `written-inquiries-2026-11-08.md`. Only the five states below changed.

| State | Before | After | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tennessee | EV and plug-in hybrid silent | Both excluded ("Not charged") | Department of Revenue reply, 2026-11-09 (ticket 2020415), citing T.C.A. 55-4-116: the fees apply only to passenger and commercial motor vehicles |
| North Dakota | EV and plug-in hybrid silent; the $20 was disclosed but left out | EV charged $20 ("+$20/yr"); plug-in hybrid stays silent | NDDOT reply, 2026-11-09, quoting N.D.C.C. 39-04-19.2(1)(c), which I also read in the statute: "An electric motorcycle road use fee of twenty dollars" |
| North Carolina | EV excluded; plug-in hybrid silent | EV stays excluded; plug-in hybrid charged at the car amount, $107.25 | NCDMV reply, 2026-11-09 (tracking 432D0CY9A7) |
| Arkansas | EV and plug-in hybrid silent | Both excluded ("Not charged") | DFA Office of Motor Vehicle reply, 2026-11-09 |
| New Jersey | EV silent | Excluded ("Not charged") | NJMVC reply, 2026-11-10: the surcharge applies to four-wheel vehicles only |

Checked in a browser on a local production build, for a motorcycle (gas, EV and plug-in hybrid registration):
- **Arkansas:** $9.50 for all three.
- **New Jersey:** $65 for gas and EV. New Jersey has no plug-in hybrid fee.
- **Tennessee:** $19.50 for all three.
- **North Dakota:** $20 gas, $40 EV, $20 plug-in hybrid.
- **North Carolina:** $29.50 gas, $29.50 EV, $136.75 plug-in hybrid.

## For Cliff's ruling, not changed here

- **New Jersey car fee.** The MVC reply gives the zero emission vehicle surcharge as $250 and says it corrects the $270 in the inquiry. The calculator charges cars $270, from the statute's $250 for July 2024 rising $10 a year. That is outside this batch and needs a ruling.
- **Arkansas wording.** The reply's sentence reads "There is no annual fee for motorcycles." Taken literally, it would mean no registration fee at all, which would conflict with the $9.50 motorcycle registration the calculator takes from DFA sources. It is read here as no EV or plug-in hybrid fee, as adopted.
- **North Dakota plug-in hybrid motorcycles.** The reply does not address them, so they stay silent.
