## North Dakota (N.D.C.C. 57-40.3, 39-04, 39-05, 39-28; NDDOT)

- Excise tax 5% of the purchase price of a vehicle bought in or outside North Dakota for use here (57-40.3-02). Confirmed.
- Purchase price (57-40.3-01(5)): the trade-in value "allowed by the person selling the motor vehicle" is deducted. The NDDOT registration manual (MV1012, rev. 07/25): "The tax is computed on the full purchase price less any trade-in allowance," and "Excise tax applies on a North Dakota vehicle purchased from an individual." Neither limits the trade-in to dealer sales. **For ruling:** the page says private trade-ins are not deductible and cites "NDDOT administrative rules and Form SFN 2872"; no rule or form text saying so was found (**unsourced** claim). The estimate and copy are unchanged.
- Gifts (57-40.3-04(5)(c); 57-40.3-07(2); NDDOT manual): a gift between husband and wife, parent and child, grandparent and grandchild, or brothers and sisters is exempt; other gifts are taxed on "the average value of similar motor vehicles" (NADA used car guide, per the manual). Inheritance and joint-tenant transfers are also exempt.
- Out of state (57-40.3-09): tax paid to another state is credited; if it is 5% or more, nothing is due; only if that state grants a similar credit. The NDDOT manual lists no reciprocity for Alaska, Delaware, Georgia, Montana, New Hampshire, and Oregon. A vehicle previously licensed by the same applicant needs no tax to re-register (57-40.3-07(1)).
- Title $5 (39-05-05). A vehicle titled in North Dakota for the first time pays a $1.50 abandoned motor vehicle disposal fee (manual; 23.1-15-11), not on motorcycles; not in the estimate (it does not apply to an in-state private transfer), disclosed. No separate lien fee found; config $0 unchanged.
- **Registration: config flat $81 -> table by year first registered.** 39-04-19(2)(a) and the NDDOT 2026 passenger fee schedule ("based on year first registered, not year model"): 3,200 to 4,499 lbs $93 (years 1 to 6), $81 (7 to 9), $69 (10 to 12), $57 (13 and later); under 3,200 lbs $73, $65, $57, $49; heavier classes more. The engine cannot combine weight and age, so the estimate uses the 3,200 to 4,499 lb class (the class the page already used) with \`feesByRegistrationYear\`, counting the model year as year 1. Monthly proration for a first registration (39-04-21) disclosed.
- **Motorcycle: config $20 -> $25.** 39-04-19(2)(c) $15 plus the $10 motorcycle safety education fee (39-28-01); the NDDOT manual: "License fee is $25."
- EV fees (39-04-19.2): $120 electric, $50 plug-in hybrid, $20 electric motorcycle. **Settled, confirmed, not reopened:** the electric motorcycle pays the separate $20 under 39-04-19.2(1)(c) (NDDOT reply of November 9, 2026); the plug-in hybrid motorcycle question is still unanswered and stays disclosed as it is.
- Deadlines (39-05-17): the seller delivers the endorsed title within 30 days of the sale; the buyer takes it to the department with the $5 fee within 30 days of receiving it, or the registration may be suspended or revoked. Residency for registration (39-04-18(2)(c)): gainfully employed and living in the state, or 90 consecutive days in the state; students and some commuters excluded.
- **Unsourced:** the local tax note says the excise tax is "in lieu of local sales taxes; no city or county taxes apply." Not checked against the sales tax chapter in this batch; left as is.
- Sources: NDDOT homepage (homepage-only flag) replaced with the NDDOT what-to-bring page and registration manual, the 2026 passenger fee schedule, and N.D.C.C. chapters 57-40.3, 39-04, 39-05, and 39-28. The N.D.C.C. label rule now reads dotted chapters ("N.D.C.C. Chapter 57-40.3").

## Alaska (AS 28.10; DMV)

- No statewide sales or use tax.
- Registration (AS 28.10.421(b); DMV 2026 chart): noncommercial passenger vehicle, pickup, truck, or van to 10,000 lbs unladen, $100 biennial; motorcycle $60. Weight brackets apply only to commercial vehicles (28.10.421(c)); age applies only to the municipal tax. Unchanged. In-person registration adds $10 (28.10.421(a)(2)); disclosed, not added.
- Title $15 and lien $15 (28.10.441; DMV out-of-state page). Unchanged.
- No state EV registration fee found in Title 28. Config $0 unchanged.
- **Municipal registration tax (AS 28.10.431):** levied by municipalities that elect it, collected by the DMV with the biennial registration, by vehicle age (model year counts as year 1; lowest bracket at 8 years). On the DMV's 2026 chart (revised 09/02/2025), noncommercial passenger vehicles, pickups, and vans pay $16 (Other Areas, oldest) to $300 (Kodiak, newest) where levied; Juneau $70 flat, Haines $22; motorcycles $4 to $40. The chart says it is collected only at issuance or renewal and not again on a change of owner during the period. Not modeled: disclosed with the range, not in the total. The FAQ named Fairbanks, which is not on the 2026 chart; replaced with the chart's locations.
- **For ruling:** a registered vehicle keeps its biennial expiration date when it changes owners (28.10.108(d)), and the DMV charges the full fee from the original expiration month when a registration has been expired less than a year. 28.10.321 has the new owner "register the vehicle as upon an original registration" with "any required registration fees." Whether a buyer of a vehicle with a current registration pays registration at transfer is not settled by these sources. The estimate keeps the full $100 ($60) and the copy says the amount at transfer may differ.
- Deadlines: title in the buyer's name within 30 days (28.10.321; DMV). A nonresident may drive on a valid out-of-state registration for 60 days, but must register within 10 days of starting work in Alaska or acting to establish residency (28.10.121; DMV registration page).
- Seniors 65 or older: one vehicle exempt from fees and taxes; the buyer of that vehicle pays full fees (DMV). Confirmed.
- **Unsourced:** "local sales tax (up to 7.5%)" in the local tax note and the FAQ's statement that cities such as Juneau or Wasilla levy sales tax on vehicle purchases. Local sales taxes are municipal and not published by the state DMV; flagged, not replaced.
- Sources: DMV homepage (homepage-only flag) replaced with the DMV registration, title, new vehicle, out-of-state transfer, and senior pages and AS Title 28; the 2026 chart kept.

## District of Columbia (D.C. Code 50-2201.03; DMV)

- **The brief describes the excise tax as tiered by weight; it is tiered by weight and city MPG.** D.C. Code 50-2201.03(j)(1) sets 6%, 7%, 8% by weight class; paragraph (1A) and the Motor Excise Tax Amendment Act of 2024 replace that with an MPG-adjusted schedule, which the DMV applies from February 17, 2025: 3,499 lbs or less, 9.0% / 5.0% / 3.1% / 2.2% / 1.5% (20 or less, 21 to 25, 26 to 30, 31 to 39, 40 or more MPG city) and 1.0% electric; 3,500 to 4,999 lbs, 10.0% / 6.0% / 4.1% / 3.2% / 2.5% / 2.0%; 5,000 lbs or more, 11.0% / 7.0% / 5.1% / 4.2% / 3.5% / 3.0%. Households that received the DC EITC may elect the weight-only rates (1A)(G). The form asks neither MPG nor exact weight, so the estimate keeps 5.0% (3,499 lbs or less, 21 to 25 MPG) and the copy states the table. Unchanged.
- Value basis: fair market value "as defined by the current National Automobile Dealers Association (NADA) Business Guide for the Eastern Region" (DMV fee page; 18 DCMR 401.16). NADA wording kept. Trade-ins do not reduce it. Confirmed.
- **Title $26 -> $30** (DMV "Vehicle Title and Excise Tax Fees": Vehicle Title Only $30). Lien recordation $20. Confirmed.
- **Registration from March 30, 2026** (DMV "Vehicle Registration Fees"): Class I (3,499 lbs or less) $70 (was $72), Class II (3,500 to 4,999) $175 (config had $115; the DMV's October 2023 column also shows $175), Class III (5,000 to 5,999) $300. **Config $72/$115 -> $70/$175**, weight label split set to 3,500 lbs. Motorcycle $52 confirmed. A brand new battery electric vehicle under 5,000 lbs pays $40 (Class V) for its first two years; hybrids do not qualify. Disclosed, not modeled.
- **For ruling:** DC collects the inspection fee ($35 for two years for a private vehicle) with registration (DMV "Vehicle Inspection Fees"); "depending on your registration date, you may have already paid." Not added; disclosed.
- **Unsourced/unclear:** the DMV registration fee page lists "Registration Card $30.00/yr" under additional fees with no explanation. Not added; flagged.
- Card payments carry a 2.5% service fee since December 15, 2025 (DMV). Disclosed.
- Exemptions (D.C. Code 50-2201.03(j); DMV exemption list, February 5, 2025): gifts between spouses, domestic partners, or parent and child, only if the vehicle already has a DC title; vehicles registered or titled elsewhere by a nonresident before establishing DC residency; surviving co-owner; divorce or separation decree or agreement; trusts where the owner is trustee.
- Title change: "If the title is not in your name already, then you will owe excise tax on the vehicle since it will be a change of ownership" (DMV "Vehicle Registration and Inspection"). No credit for tax paid to another state appears on the exemption list.
- Deadlines (DMV "Registration of a New or Used Vehicle"): new residents have 60 calendar days from the date they begin living in DC, after converting to a DC license or ID. A 45-day temporary registration is available for vehicles that must be inspected first (all title fees and excise tax paid then); a five-day temporary paper tag for an out-of-state resident who buys a used vehicle from a DC owner (DMV "Temporary Registration and Tags").
- Sources: two dead DMV links (hidden) removed; replaced with the DMV title and excise fee page, registration fee page, exemption list, new or used vehicle registration page, registration and inspection page, inspection fee page, and D.C. Code 50-2201.03 (new "D.C. Code" label).
