## B. Needs a ruling

- **Washington, regional transit authority (RTA) excise tax.** The formula is published and it is collected with registration: 1.1% of MSRP, times 100% in the first year down to 10% from the 13th ([DOL](https://dol.wa.gov/node/297)). The default car owes $158.40 (8th year, 48%). It applies only inside the Sound Transit district in King, Pierce, and Snohomish counties, so most buyers in the state do not pay it, and the site has no way to tell where an address falls. The copy already discloses it. I would keep it copy-only.
- **Massachusetts, motor vehicle excise.** The rate is statewide: $25 per $1,000 on 90%, 60%, 40%, 25%, then 10% of list price, about $75 on the default car. The city or town bills it separately, so the buyer does not pay it at registration. It already failed your test on that point and stays copy-only unless you say otherwise. The figures come from the October re-check in the site's copy. I did not re-read them today, because the mass.gov URL I tried returned a 404 and another a 403.

## Problems on live pages, found during this check (nothing changed)

1. **Nevada's copy is wrong.** It says "Registration includes an annual Governmental Services Tax," but the configured registration is a flat $33, so the estimate leaves out about $147 on the default car. This is the clearest error here, whichever way the ruling goes.
2. **Iowa's $50 is the fee only for 12-year-old cars.** It is also the floor for cars over 7 model years old. The default car owes about $314, and the page's copy does not mention a value-based fee.
3. **Four states do not mention their local vehicle tax:** Missouri and West Virginia (a receipt is needed to register), Montana (county option tax), and Virginia (locality bill, not sourced today).
4. **Age-tier states show one tier.** Montana's fee is $217, $87, or $28 by age and the site shows $87. Idaho's is $69, $57, or $45 and the site shows $45. The default car is correct in both, but newer cars are understated.
5. **New Mexico may be stale.** A [June 29, 2026 state press release](https://biz.nm.gov/newsroom/press-release-vehicle-registration-fees-weight-distance-tax-to-increas-100004574) says passenger fees rise about 25% on July 1, and the site's data is dated September 2026. This comes from a search summary; I did not open the release.

## What could change these figures

- **Pending bills:** search results showed Wyoming HB0030 (2026), Nebraska LB714 (2025), and Idaho H0533 (2026). I did not confirm whether any passed, and the Wyoming statute PDF carries no date I checked. These need checking before any of those states are built.
- **Nevada age:** the statute does not define how age is counted. One year either way moves the default car to about $105 or $189. A build would need checking against the DMV's fee calculator.
- **Iowa value:** the statute says value "as fixed by the department". The rule refers to retail list price but I did not find a published statement of how that is set for a used vehicle.
- **Sources:** several entries rest on search summaries, marked above. Each state's figures would be re-read in full before any build.

## Recommendation for the ruling

1. **Iowa and Nevada first.** They have the largest gaps between what the site shows and what the buyer pays, and Nevada's copy is wrong today.
2. **Nebraska and Wyoming next.** Both have clear statewide statutes and county collection.
3. **New Hampshire and Maine last.** The town collects, but the rates are statewide, and the copy already explains the tax.
4. **Washington, Massachusetts, and group C stay copy-only.** Add the missing disclosure for the four states in problem 3.

**Build notes if you approve any of these:**
- `registration.listPriceAnnualTax` has no minimum amount yet. Nevada, Wyoming, New Hampshire, and Maine all need one.
- Nebraska's table has about 50 brackets and an age multiplier, and the motor vehicle fee is a second charge.
- Iowa also needs weight, which the rule does not use today.
- Comparison page totals would rise for each state added, as they did for Indiana and Colorado.
