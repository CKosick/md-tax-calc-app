# Batch 14 research notes (ID, WV, HI), 2026-11-16

Working notes from the official-source re-check for guide batch 14. Items marked **For ruling** were not changed. Items marked **not primary** rest on a search summary or secondary copy.

## Idaho (ITD, Idaho State Tax Commission)

- Sales tax 6% on the total price of a private sale; collected by the county assessor (DMV) or ITD at titling. "There are no trade-in allowances in private party sales." A trade-in only counts when buying from a retailer or dealer that takes it for resale (Tax Commission, Types of Sales). Config unchanged.
- Family sale exemption: parent and child, grandparent and grandchild, and siblings, only if the family seller paid sales or use tax when buying; Form ST-133 at registration. Cousins, aunts, uncles, and foster children do not qualify.
- Gift exemption: nothing of value given back, a relationship that supports a gift, no debt assumed, and no loan owed on the vehicle; Form ST-133GT signed by both. Taking over payments is not a gift.
- Bought out of state by a resident: use tax, with credit for tax properly paid to the other state (receipt needed). New residents: no use tax on a vehicle owned more than 90 days before moving (exemption certificate); owned less than 90 days, presumed bought for use in Idaho.
- Deadline: file at a county assessor's motor vehicle office within 30 days of transfer, or a $20 late-filing penalty (ITD Vehicle Titles). VIN inspection for a vehicle coming from another state or never titled in Idaho. Odometer disclosure for vehicles newer than 2010 under 16,000 lbs.
- New residents: title and register within 90 days of moving (ITD fact sheet); residency is 90 continuous days, or earlier by declaration.
- Title $14 statutory; county title administration fees of $0 to $18.75 on top (ITD county fee table, Rev. 2-26; the table on the ITD title page and the PDF disagree for several counties). Total title fee $14 to $32.75.
- Registration (ITD fact sheet, Idaho Code): $69 one or two years old, $57 three to six, $45 seven or more. **For ruling: config charges $45 at every age.** Age counting would follow the site's usual rule unless ruled otherwise.
- Fees on every registration: $1.25 EMS and $3.00 ISP (ITD fact sheet), plus $3.75 per new plate, county administrative fees of $3.00 to $14.00 (ITD county table), and Ada County Highway District fees of $24 to $40 for some vehicles. **For ruling: none are in the estimate.**
- Motorcycle: $19 plus a $6 Motorcycle Safety Program fee = $25 (ITD fact sheet). **For ruling: config $19.**
- EV $140 and plug-in hybrid $75 per year confirmed (ITD fact sheet). Idaho is a postal written-inquiry state (motorcycle EV fees); nothing here changes that question.
- Sources: ITD and Tax Commission homepages replaced with the ITD title and registration pages, the fact sheet, the county fee table, and the Tax Commission private-party page.

## West Virginia (DMV, W. Va. Code)

- **Rate: 6%, not 5%.** W. Va. Code 11-15-3c applies the consumers sales tax to motor vehicle sales, including "isolated transactions between individuals" and "casual and occasional sales between individuals", at five percent, rising "to six percent of the sales price for purchases of motor vehicles made on and after" July 1, 2017. The DMV title page says "a 6% titling sales tax on vehicles with a net purchase price over $500 (or a flat $30.00 if the net price is under $500)". 17A-3-4 still sets the 5% privilege tax on certification of title; the DMV collects the 6% sales tax. **Config 5% -> 6%, with a $30 minimum (`minExciseTax`).**
- Valuation (DMV title page; 11-15-3c directs rules on a minimum taxable value and fair market value): a private sale below 50% of NADA Clean Loan value needs a notarized bill of sale signed by both, with copies of both driver's licenses, or tax is assessed on the NADA Clean Loan value; a vehicle two years old or newer bought from a non-dealer is taxed on NADA Clean Loan value whatever the price. Disclosed, not modeled.
- **For ruling: private trade-ins.** 11-15-3c(b): "so much of the sale price or consideration as is represented by the exchange of other vehicles on which the tax ... has been paid by the purchaser shall be deducted ... whether the motor vehicle be new or used." The text is not limited to dealers. The DMV adds that the trade-in must be titled in West Virginia in the applicant's name (in the same name as the vehicle bought). Config `tradeInDeductible: false`, with copy citing a regulation for dealer-only; unchanged.
- Gifts: Affidavit of Transfer of Ownership Without Consideration (DMV-5-TR) signed by both; none needed between parent and child or spouses for a vehicle titled in West Virginia (17A-3-4(b)(2), tax previously paid). Without the affidavit, tax on full book value. If a lien is recorded on the new title, tax is 6% of the lien amount. 17A-3-4(b)(1) taxes gifts at market value under the privilege tax "unless specifically exempted"; the DMV page describes the affidavit as the way a no-consideration transfer avoids the sales tax. **Not settled from the statute text alone;** the copy follows the DMV page.
- Out of state: the DMV does not give credit for sales tax paid to another state (title page, non-resident purchases).
- New residents: no titling tax on a vehicle already titled in the applicant's name in another state, acquired while not a resident; title and register within 30 days of establishing residency (DMV new residents page). Safety inspection within 10 days of titling.
- Inspection: a vehicle bought in West Virginia without a valid sticker must be inspected within 10 days; inspection every 24 months.
- No statute or DMV page giving a private buyer a day count to title was found.
- Title $15, lien $10 confirmed (DMV). Registration Class A $51.50 confirmed (DMV fee brochure, Rev. 05/2024, includes $1 litter and $0.50 insurance enforcement fees). **Motorcycle $25 -> $16** (brochure, Class G standard plate; matches the $16 in the DMV's November 9 email reply). EV +$200 and hybrid +$100 confirmed; the brochure says "hybrid vehicles", which may include hybrids that do not plug in (the form has no choice for those). None of this changes the written inquiry's question (whether motorcycles pay the EV and plug-in hybrid fees).
- Personal property tax receipt needed to renew or transfer a registration; disclosed already.
- Sources: transportation.wv.gov homepage replaced with the DMV title, new resident, and registration pages, the fee brochure, and W. Va. Code 11-15-3c and 17A-3-4.

## Hawaii (HRS 249, Honolulu CSD, Hawaii County VRL, Maui County, DOTAX)

- No GET or use tax on a casual sale between individuals. A used car bought from an individual out of state for personal use owes no use tax, but Form G-27 certifies that to the county. A vehicle bought from an out-of-state dealer owes use tax of 4.5% of landed value, with credit for sales tax paid to the other state up to 4.5% (DOTAX Use Tax brochure, 2025). New residents: personal automobiles acquired outside Hawaii while not a resident, for use and substantially used outside Hawaii, are exempt; acquired less than three months before import, presumed bought for use in Hawaii. G-27 is required to register current, previous, and subsequent model years (Maui and Hawaii County pages).
- State registration fee $46 (HRS 249-31). State weight tax 1.75 cents a pound up to 4,000 lbs net weight, 2.00 cents to 7,000, 2.25 cents to 10,000, $300 over (HRS 249-33). Both collected by the county with the county fee.
- Honolulu (CSD weight-tax waiver guidance, 2023; dealer resources page, 2026): county registration fee $20, Highway Beautification Fee $10 from June 1, 2026 (was $7), emblem $0.50, new plate $5, plus the county weight tax. Honolulu county weight tax rate: **not primary** (ROH 41-17.1 not read; secondary sources give 7 cents a pound for passenger vehicles).
- Hawaii County: county weight tax 1.25 cents a pound, $12 minimum, per the county fee page as summarized by search (**not primary**; the page redirected when opened). Late renewal penalty $16 ($8 county, $8 state). Maui and Kauai county rates: **not found on an official page** (pages bot-blocked or silent); Kauai's penalty is 20% of the county and state weight tax.
- **For ruling: the calculator's registration is $46, the state fee alone.** It leaves out the state weight tax (about $61 for a 3,500 lb car), every county's registration fee and weight tax, and Honolulu's beautification and emblem fees. The modeled figure fits no county. Options: model the statewide parts (fee plus weight tax at an assumed weight) and disclose county amounts, or add a county choice.
- Copy said Honolulu's county weight tax is 7.0 cents a pound and counties charge "up to 7.0 cents"; neither is from a primary source, so the copy now says county rates vary. The state 1.75 cents a pound (to 4,000 lbs) is confirmed.
- EV fee: since July 1, 2025, HRS 249-31's $50 surcharge names alternative fuel vehicles, and battery electric vehicles of three or more wheels pay the road usage charge (a $50 flat option through June 30, 2028), as recorded in the November 7 motorcycle notes. Honolulu's guidance lists an "electric/hybrid/alternate fuel vehicle fee ($50)". The statute says the surcharge is collected beginning with the first registration renewal. Config +$50; unchanged. Nothing here changes the written inquiry's question (whether motorcycles pay).
- Transfer: $10 transfer fee within 30 days of the seller's transfer date, $50 penalty after (Honolulu CSD; the page is dated). Current safety inspection required. Seller files a Notice of Transfer within 10 days.
- Sources: hidot homepage and the Honolulu inquiry app replaced with HRS 249-31 and 249-33, the Honolulu dealer resources and weight-tax guidance pages, the Hawaii County new registration page, and the DOTAX use tax brochure.
