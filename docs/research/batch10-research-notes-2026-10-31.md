## Wisconsin (WisDOT, DOR)
- Title $214.50 (original or transfer); $0 for transfer to a surviving spouse, registered domestic partner, or qualifying family member; lien $10. https://wisconsindot.gov/pages/dmv/vehicles/title-plates/titles.aspx
- Registration (on or after Oct 1, 2025): automobile $85; motorcycle $23 biennial; + $175 EV, $75 hybrid surcharge; wheel tax where kept. https://wisconsindot.gov/Pages/dmv/vehicles/title-plates/fee-chart.aspx
- Apply immediately for title on purchase/gift/new residency; display plates or temp plate within two business days. https://wisconsindot.gov/Pages/online-srvcs/title-vehicle/emvpublicdefault.aspx
- Purchase price = money paid minus any trade-in allowance; even trade exempt; gift and heir exempt; family: spouse, ex-spouse (decree), parent, child (incl. child's spouse), mother/father-in-law, stepparent, stepchild, sole proprietor; family exemption only with a current Wisconsin title; new resident exempt if bought outside WI while nonresident 90+ days before moving. https://wisconsindot.gov/Pages/online-srvcs/title-vehicle/faqs-purchase-price.aspx
- Rates: state 5%; 0.5% county tax in 70 counties (none in Waukesha, Winnebago); Milwaukee County 0.9% (1/24); City of Milwaukee 2% (1/24). Range 0% to 2.9%. https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/FAQS/pcs-taxrates.aspx (Jan 16, 2026)
- Dead link: https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/FAQS/pcs-motorveh.aspx

## Colorado (DMV, Taxation)
- Title transfer $7.20; adding a lien: lien filing fees $40 + $7.20 title. Private sale: properly endorsed title, odometer (2011+ models, first 20 years), SVID (DR 2841), bill of sale recommended, VIN inspection if from out of state. https://dmv.colorado.gov/title
- Register within 60 days of purchase; new residents within 90 days; late fee $25 per month up to $100. https://dmv.colorado.gov/taxes-and-fees
- Registration parts: base fee by empty weight (C.R.S. 42-3-306(2)(b)(I): $6 to 2,000 lb, +$0.20 per 100 lb to 4,500; >4,500 $12.50 + $0.60/100), age fee $12 (<7 yrs), $10 (7-9), $7 (10+) from July 1, 2026; road safety $19.30 (2,001-5,000 lb), $12.30 motorcycle (to Aug 31, 2027); bridge $18 (2,001-5,000), $13 motorcycle; clerk $4; EMS $2; emissions county $0.70; statewide air $0.50; MIIDB $0.10; POST $1; year tab $0.59; plates $7.38 embossed set on issuance; emissions area $1.50. Motorcycle base $3. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_42-3-306
- EV/PHEV: road usage fee $63.05 ("Plug-In Electric Vehicle"); EMV road usage fee 7/1/26-6/30/27: BEV $26, PHEV $13.
- SOT: Class C taxable value 85% of MSRP; 2.1%, 1.5%, 1.2%, 0.9%, then 0.45% years 5-9, $3 from year 10. Not modeled.
- Taxation (Sales & Use Tax Topics: Motor Vehicle Sales): private sales, buyer pays state and local taxes to the county clerk at registration; trade-in FMV excluded (no dealer limit); gift recipient not liable but giver must have paid tax, clerk needs receipts; lien assumption is a taxable sale; new residents: no use tax on vehicles acquired outside CO and brought in. https://tax.colorado.gov/sales-use-tax-topics-motor-vehicle-sales
- Local rates: DR 1002 is a Data Studio report (city, county, special district, home-rule listed separately); no reliable statewide combined range extracted.

## South Carolina (SCDMV, SC Code)
- dmv.sc.gov (scdmvonline.com redirects). Fees: title $15; most passenger cars $40; 64: $38; 65+/disability $36; motorcycles $10; hybrids $60 + registration; EVs $120 + registration; most fees biennial; IMF 5% max $500; $250 one-time for vehicles previously registered in another state; late registration penalties from day 46. https://dmv.sc.gov/fees
- Buying: register within 45 days; title signed with price, odometer; Form 400; IMF; property tax bill paid at county first; plates do not transfer between people. https://dmv.sc.gov/vehicle-owners/buying-or-selling-a-car
- S.C. Code 56-3-627(C): private sale IMF 5% of fair market value, max $500; FMV = total purchase price less trade-in, or national used-value publication less trade-in; excluded: immediate family (spouse, parents, children, sisters, brothers, grandparents, grandchildren), heir/legatee/distributee; (D) $250 if first titled in another state (not active duty military). https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t56c003.php
- New residents: 45 days (county pages; DMV Moving to SC page).
- Dead link: https://www.scdmvonline.com/Vehicle-Owners/Titles-and-Registration
