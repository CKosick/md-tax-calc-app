## Washington (DOR, DOL, RCW)
- State rate on motor vehicles is 7.0%: 6.5% retail sales/use tax (RCW 82.08.020(1)) plus the motor vehicle sales/use tax, 0.5% from Jan 1, 2026 (0.3% through 2025). https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/other-taxes/motor-vehicle-salesuse-tax . DOL's use tax page still says 0.3% (stale): https://dol.wa.gov/vehicles-and-boats/vehicles/taxes-and-fees/use-tax
- Local rates Q4 2026 (Oct 1 to Dec 31, 2026): 1.1% (Klickitat Co. unincorporated) to 4.2% (Edmonds, Lynnwood). DOR file Q426_Excel_LSU-rates-alpha.xlsx from https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/sales-use-tax-rates/local-sales-use-tax
- Luxury motor vehicle tax: 8% of selling price + trade-in minus the deduction, $102,000 for Jul 1, 2026 to Jun 30, 2027 ($100,000 Jan to Jun 2026). Applies at DOL registration if not collected. https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/other-taxes/luxury-motor-vehicle-tax
- Value: use tax on fair market value; price accepted if no more than 20% below FMV or FMV under $7,500 (WAC 458-20-17802). Lower value can be documented (pricing guide, repair estimate, appraisal). DOR REV 32 2511 (11/2/21): https://dor.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2022-02/usetaxfactsforvehicletransactions.pdf
- Gift/inheritance: exempt if previous owner paid WA sales or use tax and no consideration; loans generally disqualify. DOL adds: exempt if giver owned it 7+ years in a state or province with sales tax; Oregon gift pays use tax.
- Title: $15 application (RCW 46.17.100; $40 for dealer sales from Oct 1, 2026, $15 for all others) + $6.50 filing (46.17.005) + $18 service (46.17.040) = $39.50.
- Tabs: license fee $30 (46.17.350, car and motorcycle) + weight fee $35 up to 4,000 lb, $65 to 6,000 (46.17.365, registrations due on/after Jan 1, 2026; motorcycles included) + $6 filing + $11 service + $0.75 license service fee (46.17.025) = $82.75 / $112.75. DOL fee page still shows "$43.25 basic fee" (old filing/service amounts). Other local fees (RTA excise, TBD) vary.
- EV: $100 + $50 (46.17.323) + $75 transportation electrification fee (46.17.324) = $225 at renewal, for plug-ins with 30+ mile battery range. Hybrids/alt fuel otherwise: $75 (46.17.324(2)).
- Plates: buyer must get new plates (except spouse/family gift/inheritance). Original issue $50 per plate + $2 reflectivity per plate (46.17.200); motorcycle $20 + $2. Plate transfer $10.
- Transfer within 15 days; $50 on day 16, $2/day after, up to $125. Documents: signed title, Vehicle Title Application (sign before agent/notary), odometer disclosure (2011+), Vehicle/Vessel Bill of Sale. https://dol.wa.gov/vehicles-and-boats/vehicles/buying-and-selling-vehicle/buy-and-register-vehicle
- New residents: 30 days for license and registration; WA license first. https://dol.wa.gov/moving-washington/vehicle-registration-and-plates . Use tax exempt if acquired and used while a resident of another state more than 90 days before entering WA (RCW 82.12.0251(3)).

## Arizona (ARS, ADOR; azdot.gov is behind a Cloudflare check, not bypassed)
- No tax on casual sales between individuals (ADOR "Understanding use tax"). https://azdor.gov/individual-income-tax-filing-assistance/understanding-use-tax
- ARS 28-2003: title $4; registration $8, motorcycle $9. ARS 49-551: $1.50 air quality fee per registration. ARS 28-2007: highway safety fee ended after June 30, 2021.
- VLT (ARS 28-5801): first 12 months value = 60% of manufacturer's base retail price, rate $2.80 per $100; each later year value 16.25% lower, rate $2.89 per $100; minimum $10.
- Title application within 15 days (ARS 28-2058(A)(1)(d), 28-2051). Seller keeps or surrenders plates (28-2058(A)(1)(c)).
- No lien fee found in statute (old config had $4 unsourced). No EV fee in 28-2003.

## Tennessee (DOR clerk manual June 2025, DOR help articles)
- Private (occasional) sales are taxable: 7% state on full price, local rate on first $1,600, state single article 2.75% on $1,600.01 to $3,200. Manual: https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/revenue/documents/tax_manuals/june-2025/County-Clerk-Tax-Manual.pdf ; VTR-34.
- Local rates: county map legend 2.00%, 2.25%, 2.50%, 2.75% (cap 2.75%). https://www.tn.gov/revenue/taxes/sales-and-use-tax/local-sales-tax/local-sales-tax-rates-map.html
- Exempt: spouses, siblings, lineal relatives and their spouses; divorce; gifts with no consideration (Affidavit of Non-Dealer Transfers). https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/revenue/title-and-registration/vehicle-titling/taxation-cars-boats.html
- Price under 75% of book: affidavit required; tax on actual price with affidavit, else on 100% of FMV.
- Trade-in credit only when a dealer gives it on the invoice.
- Title $5.50 state + $8.50 clerk = $14; lien $11. https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/revenue/title-and-registration/vehicle-titling/lien-notation.html
- Plate $26.50, motorcycle $19.50 (VR-4). EV $200 for renewals before Jan 1, 2027, $274 in 2027; hybrid and plug-in hybrid $100 (VR-5). County wheel taxes vary.
- New residents: register within 30 days (VR-6, TCA 55-4-101(b)(2)); bona fide relocation imports personal vehicle without use tax; credit for other state's tax.
